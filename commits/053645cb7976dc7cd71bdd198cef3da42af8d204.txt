feat(editor): play mode — scene restart, gated input, hidden overlay, pointer lock (#58)
The editor gains a play/stop switch. Entering restarts the scene from its
authored state, arms the scene's game camera rig and requests pointer lock;
leaving releases the lock, unloads the rig and reinstalls the editor orbit
camera. EditorUI reads the state to hide the ImGui overlay (no draw-data
submitted) and to report no capture, so GameInputDriver stops suppressing the
polled input and the character controller plus the camera rig receive it. The
toolbar Play (F5) button and the F5/Escape hotkeys drive it; the selection is
remembered by name and restored on exit.

- EditorPlayMode: play/editor switch, scene restart via ISceneManager, pointer
  lock arming (re-armed each frame so a mid-play camera swap keeps the cursor)
  and the editor camera restore.
- IFollowCameraRig gains LockPointer so play mode arms the rig through the
  contract, not the concrete rig.
- EditorUI: capture flags read false while playing, Render skips the panels and
  drops the draw-data, toolbar play button, selection restore.
- Tests: EditorPlayModeTests (restart, arm/release, a fresh rig per entry, key
  handling, camera-swap re-arm) and EditorUIPlayModeTests (overlay hidden, gate
  open, selection restore, pointer lock).
- ADR docs/adr/editor-play-mode.md and a CONTEXT.md glossary entry.
