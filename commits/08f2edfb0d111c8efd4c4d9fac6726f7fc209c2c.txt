feat(character): editable capsule volume and an upright FBX bind pose (#25)
Expose the kinematic capsule's Radius/Height as mutable, clamped properties on
KinematicCapsuleBody and pass them through on CharacterControllerComponent and
KinematicBodyComponent, so the reflection inspector can resize the character's
physical volume live (height stays >= 2*radius, feet stay put).

Fix the FBX importer's skeleton mapping: keep the non-bone armature ancestors
(a Blender Armature node) as weightless bones and set GlobalInverseTransform to
the inverse of the mesh node's world transform. Without that the bind pose
dropped the armature rotation and the skinned knight was drawn lying down. The
demo now imports Resources/FBX/knight.fbx instead of the stale Json/knight.json,
and the binary asset cache schema is bumped because the importer mapping changed.

Tests: capsule resize updates the live volume and the next step uses it; the
demo knight is upright (skinned bounds) and grounded after settling; walking
into the demo crate is blocked.
