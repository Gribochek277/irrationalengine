feat(combat): health, damage, hit-flash and death (#53)
Add the combat flow shared by the player and enemies:

- Pure `Health` model (current/max, TakeDamage, Damaged/Died events,
  overkill and ignored-after-death) plus `DamageInfo`/`DamageResult`.
- `IHealthComponent`/`HealthComponent` as the scene-object seam.
- `IDamageService`/`DamageService`: resolves the target's health by
  interface, applies the hit, flashes on every applied hit and on a
  lethal hit disables the `IGameplayController` seam and fires the
  state machine's "death" transition.
- `IHitFlashComponent`/`HitFlashComponent`: frame-counted diffuse and
  ambient material override that preserves the alpha and restores the
  authored colours; `IHitFlashUpdater`/`HitFlashUpdater` age it once
  per frame, run by SceneManager after the state-machine stage.
- `IGameplayController` one-member enable seam for the character
  controller (#59).
- SceneObject wires the material component into the hit flash; the
  composition root registers the damage service and hit-flash stage.
- Demo scene gives the Knight health + hit flash and binds `H` to a
  damage request, so the flash is visible without the game host.
- ADR + CONTEXT.md glossary entries.

Unit tests cover damage, lethal, overkill, ignored-after-death,
invalid damage, event order, the flash countdown/restore/re-trigger,
the death reaction order and the scene-object wiring.
