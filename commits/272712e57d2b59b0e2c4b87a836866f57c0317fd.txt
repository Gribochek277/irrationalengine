fix(audio): case-sensitive clip cache — distinct files stay distinct (PR #88 review P2)
StringComparer.OrdinalIgnoreCase treated 'Shot.wav' and 'shot.wav' as one
cache key; on a case-sensitive filesystem (Linux, primary dev platform)
those are two different files, so the second LoadClip silently returned
the first clip's audio. Use StringComparer.Ordinal — GetFullPath already
normalizes separators, and on Windows a case-variant path just re-decodes
into a second cache entry (safe). Regression test loads two case-variant
files with different PCM and asserts distinct clips and data (skips on
case-insensitive filesystems, where the aliasing cannot occur).
