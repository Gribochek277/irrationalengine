fix: use the animated shader for the glTF duck to share the scene's shader program
Both material components received the same injected IShaderProgram. The knight
prepares it with the animated shaders and flips _stagesReady, so when the duck
loaded its static shaders afterwards PrepareShaders early-returned and
_shaderModules (2) fell out of sync with _storedShaders (4), throwing
IndexOutOfRange in BuildPipeline. Reusing the animated shader pair keeps the
counts in sync (duplicate shader names are de-duplicated) and renders the
static duck correctly via the total-weight path.
