fix(physics): clamp per-step vertical motion to stop ceiling tunnelling
Review fix (PR #89, P3): X and Z were clamped by MaxHorizontalStep but
delta.Y was not, and the solver's rising branch has no sweep ray — a
200 m/s launch passed straight through a 0.05-thick ceiling with
HitCeiling = false. KinematicCapsuleBody now clamps delta.Y by a new
MoveAndSlideSettings.MaxVerticalStep (default 1.5, same as horizontal);
keeping the clamp at or below the capsule height means a step can never
clear a box top end-to-end, so the post-move overlap test always sees it.
Downward stays ray-grounded as before.

Regression test: a 200 m/s launch under a 0.05-thick slab hits the
ceiling on the first clamped ascent (flag set, upward velocity zeroed)
and never rises above the slab's bottom face over 60 frames.
