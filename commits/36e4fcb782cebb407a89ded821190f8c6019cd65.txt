feat: gizmo Unity handle set — plane squares, uniform cube, screen ring (#65)
Add Unity-parity handles to the gizmo rig:
- Translate plane squares on the XY/YZ/XZ axis pairs: ray-picked quads at
  the pair corner; drag moves the object 1:1 in the pair plane (ray-plane
  intersection through the pivot, camera-plane fallback when edge-on).
- Scale center cube: world-axis AABB at the pivot; uniform scale from the
  radial relative mouse delta (anchor-position compensated).
- Rotate screen ring: camera-oriented ring at a larger radius; rotation
  around the view axis captured at grab, incremental screen-plane angle,
  orbit-position corrected.

A single GizmoHandle enum now identifies every handle across picking,
hover, drag state and the overlay; picking stays depth-first (nearest ray
depth wins). The bars' pick boxes are clamped so they never reach into the
cube region; plane squares use a smaller pick margin so they cannot steal
single-axis grabs from off-axis cameras. HoverHandle/ActiveHandle exposed
for the cursor and highlight; both backends are unaffected (the rig is
ImGui-drawn editor code). Supersedes #27 (camera-plane center translate).
