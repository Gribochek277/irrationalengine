feat: camera IsMain via ICameraRegistry and material render strategy for glTF textures
Camera (Unity-style IsMain toggle):
- ICameraRegistry + DefaultCameraRegistry own the main-camera role: at most one main, at least one main while cameras exist, no camera -> scene not rendered.
- ICamera.IsMain on both Camera and OrbitCamera, delegating to the registry (no own state).
- DefaultScene registers/unregisters cameras on add/remove and sets main on load; dead IScene.MainCamera setter removed.
- Renderers resolve the main camera from the registry each frame and skip the scene draw when it is null.
- EditorUI orbit-cam swap preserves the main role.

Duck texture (material render strategy):
- IMaterialRenderStrategy + DiffuseMaterialStrategy/PbrMaterialStrategy/MaterialStrategyResolver in IeCoreEntities.Materials; renderers consume materials through the resolver via DI.
- DefaultScene now uses the PbrMaterial the glTF importer built (named after its glTF name, not 'Duck') with its embedded BaseColorTexture; checkerboard is only a literal fallback when no base-color texture exists.

Tests & ADRs:
- New IeCore.Tests project: registry invariants, strategy resolver, IsMain delegation, DefaultScene camera register/unregister.
- FakeCameraRegistry + EditorUI swap-preserves-main test; renderer contract tests updated for the new ctor params.
- ADRs: docs/adr/camera-registry-is-main.md, docs/adr/material-render-strategy.md.
