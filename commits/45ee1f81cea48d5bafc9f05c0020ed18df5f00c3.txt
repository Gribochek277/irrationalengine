feat(animation): cross-fade, 1D locomotion blend and bone sockets (#41)
Blend two clips on the skeleton instead of the final matrices: each source's
local bone transform is sampled and interpolated, then the parent chain and the
skinning formula are applied once, so a clip switch no longer jumps the pose.

- IAnimationComponent.CrossFadeTo(index/name, seconds) ramps a fade between the
  outgoing and incoming clips; completing the ramp promotes the incoming clip at
  its current playhead. The demo's animation hotkeys now cross-fade.
- LocomotionBlend maps character speed onto the idle -> walk -> run axis
  (LocomotionBlendSettings names the clips and full-blend speeds);
  SetLocomotionSpeed drives the pose. Playheads are kept per clip.
- IBoneSocketProvider.TryGetBoneSocketWorldMatrix returns a named bone's animated
  world frame for prop attachment, using the global transforms captured while the
  blended pose is evaluated.
- Pure helpers MatrixBlend, LocomotionBlend and BonePoseEvaluator.EvaluateBlended
  are unit-tested (matrix lerp, socket on a rotated object).
- ADR records the skeleton-vs-matrices and narrow-socket-contract decisions; the
  skinning pipeline and both backends are unchanged.
