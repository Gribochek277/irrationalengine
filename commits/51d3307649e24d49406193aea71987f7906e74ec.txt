fix(serialization): bake the editor camera's view into a saved scene's camera (#39)
EditorUI.Init replaces the authored rig with the editor's OrbitCamera on the
scene's camera object, and CameraComponentCodec.Capture had no branch for it:
every editor save wrote a bare { "kind": "Fps" }, so a scene loaded with
--game sat at the world origin (inside the demo ground) and rendered nothing.

Decompose the camera's view instead. CameraRigMath.DecomposeView(view) is the
inverse of View(eye, forward): it recovers the eye from the rotation/
translation pair, takes the look direction as the negated third column of the
rotation part, and derives pitch = asin(forward.Y) and yaw = atan2(-forward.X,
-forward.Z), passing both through ClampPitch/WrapYaw so the pose is always
expressible by a yaw/pitch rig. Roll is dropped by design and a degenerate or
non-finite matrix yields the zero pose rather than NaN.

The capture fallback then consumes only ICamera.GetViewMatrix() -- no editor
type, which the dependency direction forbids anyway -- and writes the
decomposed position/yaw/pitch with the FPS defaults the restore side assumes.

Tests cover the inverse (including a wrapped yaw, a pitch at the clamp and a
non-identity eye), a rolled view, degenerate matrices, a stub ICamera round
trip and the editor's OrbitCamera round trip. The chosen semantics are
recorded in the camera glossary and the game-camera-rigs ADR: preserving the
suspended authored rig across a save is a deliberate follow-up.
