refactor(components): audit placement, single responsibility and duplication (#28)
Audit of the component layer written by tickets 08, 13, 14, 16-21 and 23,
with the mechanical findings fixed:

- Move IHealthComponent, IHitFlashComponent and IWeaponComponent to
  IeCoreInterfaces/Combat, and IStateMachineComponent to
  IeCoreInterfaces/Gameplay, so every component contract mirrors the domain
  folder that owns its implementation (Combat / Gameplay). Character, enemy
  and audio component contracts already mirrored theirs.
- Add a ComponentName constant to every component contract and replace the
  duplicated key literals in the components, the frame stages, SceneObject's
  load wiring, the serializer and its codecs, both renderers, the render
  pipeline, the editor catalog/drawers and the debug overlays.
- Extract IeUtils.AngleMath (Wrap/Step); CameraRigMath.WrapYaw and
  CharacterControllerMath.WrapAngle/StepAngle delegate to it, so the
  [-pi, pi) convention has one implementation.
- Extract SceneCameraLookup.FindFollowRig, shared by the character, weapon
  and enemy stages instead of three private copies.
- Drop four stale "TODO: Add caching" comments on ModelComponent accessors
  that already cache.

docs/adr/component-architecture-audit.md records the component and stage
tables, the verdicts and the deliberate follow-ups (camera-basis extraction,
ICamera/ISceneMutable placement, collider/cull composition, multi-type files
outside the component layer).

Build and the full suite (1189 tests) stay green.
