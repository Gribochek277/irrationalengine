feat(combat): enemy dummy AI — patrol, line of sight and return fire (#66)
A dummy target with a minimal brain: it walks a patrol route while it cannot
see its target, notices the target by distance plus a line-of-sight ray through
the scene physics queries, and returns fire with a hitscan while the target
stays visible. Both sides of the demo can die.

- EnemyDummyComponent: patrol movement, perception with range hysteresis,
  return fire through IHitscanEffects and IDamageService, and a fire cooldown.
- EnemySight / PatrolRoute: the pure perception and patrol rules.
- EnemyDummyStateMachine: the declarative patrol / attack / death table,
  driven through the shared IGameplayStateMachine; the dummy implements
  IGameplayController so the damage service disables it on death.
- EnemyDummyUpdater: a frame stage run after the weapon stage; dormant while
  the scene's camera is not a game rig.
- SceneObject wires the object's state machine into the dummy on load.
- Demo: the target dummy patrols and shoots back at the knight.
- Tests: perception, patrol, firing, LOS blocking, state transitions, stage
  wiring and the demo end-to-end.
- ADR docs/adr/enemy-dummy-ai.md and CONTEXT.md glossary entries.
