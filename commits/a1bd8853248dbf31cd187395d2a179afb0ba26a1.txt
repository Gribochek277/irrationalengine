feat: gizmo rotate drag — fixed drag plane, incremental angle, degeneracy fallbacks (#56)
The rotate drag re-evaluated the face-on/edge-on threshold every frame,
returned a zero delta on degenerate ray/plane intersections (freezing at
screen edges), and measured the angle from the grab point, which a camera
move mid-drag could warp.

- SelectRotatePlane: pick the ring plane or the camera-facing plane once
  at BeginDrag — no mid-drag plane switches, no angle jump at the boundary.
- ComputeRotatePoint: intersect the cursor ray with the fixed plane; fall
  back to the other plane (then the pivot) instead of freezing on a zero.
- ComputeRotateDelta(previous, current, pivot, axis): signed angle between
  successive drag points — incremental, so only the world points matter.
- ComputeRotateDeltaCameraOnEdge: three.js tangential model for edge-on
  rings; trackball angle when the tangent degenerates (view along axis).
- GizmoSystem stores the plane and the previous point, updating it each frame.
- Sign test matrix 3 axes × 6 octants, camera-move incremental test, plane
  classification and fallback tests.

Scratch card 03 marked done; the ring hit-zone closing gap was already
fixed by #72's 3D ray-pick (TryRing closes the polyline).
