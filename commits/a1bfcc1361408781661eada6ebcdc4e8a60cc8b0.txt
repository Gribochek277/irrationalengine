feat(editor): draw kinematic capsule bodies in the collider debug view (#32)
The "Show colliders" overlay drew only IColliderComponent world boxes, so a
character's KinematicCapsuleBody was invisible and its configured size could
not be read without opening the inspector.

- ColliderVolumeMath.Resolve is the single pure seam that returns a
  ColliderVolume (box and/or capsule); the capsule is resolved through the
  IKinematicBodyComponent contract, never a concrete type or component name.
- CapsuleWireframeMath.Edges returns the centre rings, vertical silhouette
  edges and hemisphere arcs of a capsule in the same (Start, End) form as
  ColliderBoxMath.Edges; a zero-core capsule degrades to a sphere without
  duplicate or zero-length segments.
- ColliderOverlay draws boxes and capsules for every object (selected or
  not) and labels the selected capsule with its radius and height.
- Picking stays box-only; SceneViewMathTests covers the new geometry, the
  volume seam and the size label.
