fix: address PR #86 review — cube pick margin, plane-square quad order, housekeeping
P1 must-fixes (glm-5.3 review):
- TryCube applied the pixel hit margin twice (unit-space CubeHalfSize +
  world-space margin multiplied by scale again), making the pick box grow
  quadratically with camera distance: it reached past the bar-shaft clamp
  and stole inner-bar-shaft clicks, breaking the 'bars win along their
  visible shafts' invariant; at close range it undershot the 12px margin.
  Now HitRadiusWorld(CubeHalfSize, scale) — margin in world space exactly
  once — matching the rendered/ShowHitAreas geometry and the bar clamp at
  every rig scale.
- DrawPlaneSquare emitted quad corners in diagonal order, rendering a
  self-crossing bowtie polygon. Corner generation moved to a pure
  GizmoManipulator.PlaneSquareCorners (perimeter order 0,1,3,2) so the
  overlay fills a real square and the ordering is unit-testable.

P3 nice-to-haves (done in the same pass):
- GizmoManipulator.cs held two top-level types; GizmoAxis split into its
  own file (one type per file).
- ComputeOrbitPositionAxis dropped its unused startRotation parameter
  (GizmoSystem + manipulator test call sites updated).
- Restored the trailing newline at the end of GizmoHandlePicking.cs.

Regression tests: far-rig-scale picking keeps the cube inside its region
while the inner bar shaft stays grabbable as the axis (scale ~7, margin
would previously overshoot ~4x), and the plane-square corner sequence has
no diagonal jumps.
