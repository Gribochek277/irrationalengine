feat: back-to-front sort of alpha-blended draw calls (#69)
The Sort stage now splits culled calls into a depth-writing pass (opaque +
mask, grouped by material slot as before) and an alpha-blend pass drawn
after it, sorted back-to-front by squared distance from the camera to the
object center. RenderPipeline recovers the camera position from the view
matrix once per frame and passes it to the sorter, so ordering stays pure
and identical for both backends.

Blend pipeline state (blending on, depth write off) and the mask cutoff
shaders already landed in #48/#55; this closes the ordering gap they
explicitly deferred.
