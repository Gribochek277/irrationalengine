feat(scene): register gameplay-spawned objects through a runtime seam (#31)
Hitscan tracers and muzzle flashes were ordinary scene objects, so they
appeared in the editor hierarchy, were written by a Save and outlived a
play-mode restart because the effects singleton kept its pool pointing at
objects the reload had discarded.

Add IRuntimeSceneObjects in IeCoreInterfaces.Scene: DefaultScene tracks
objects registered through it, excludes them from authored views, and
releases them — bumping a generation — on unload and scene load. The scene
serializer and the editor hierarchy skip runtime objects. SceneHitscanEffects
registers its capped pool through the seam and rebuilds it when the scene or
its generation changes, so a session that starts after the pool filled still
draws. The seam lives in the scene domain, so gameplay no longer names the
editor's ISceneMutable and runs in game-host mode.

Record every scene-object creation site and its verdict in
docs/adr/runtime-scene-objects.md.
