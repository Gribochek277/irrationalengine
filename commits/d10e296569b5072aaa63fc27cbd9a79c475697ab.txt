feat(combat): hitscan weapon — tracer, muzzle flash and cooldown (#61)
A hitscan weapon shared by the player and the enemy AI: the Fire action
casts a ray from the camera eye (FPS) or the muzzle bone socket (third
person) through the scene physics queries, damages a hit target through
the damage service and shows a frame-counted tracer and muzzle flash.
Damage, range, cooldown and magazine/ammo are component fields.

- WeaponAimResolver: pure origin selection (camera vs muzzle socket).
- HitscanResolver: pure hit resolution that skips the shooter's own
  collider, resuming a start-inside self hit past the collider diagonal.
- HitscanWeaponComponent: firing state, damage and effect dispatch;
  TryFire is the programmatic path for the enemy AI.
- SceneHitscanEffects: pooled scene-object tracers/flashes using a
  shared untextured unlit cube, so both backends draw them through the
  ordinary Collect -> Draw path.
- WeaponUpdater runs last in SceneManager, after the physics stage and
  the scene's camera tick.
- SceneObject wires the animation component's bone socket into a weapon.
- Demo: the knight carries a weapon and a target dummy stands ahead.
- Tests: origin selection, hit resolution, self-skip, cooldown, ammo,
  tracer/flash placement, socket wiring, weapon stage and demo firing.
- ADR docs/adr/hitscan-weapon.md and CONTEXT.md glossary entries.
