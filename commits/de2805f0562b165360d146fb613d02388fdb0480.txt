refactor: mirror the renderer seam on the OpenGL side — IOpenGlRenderer + renderer-owned editor renderer
Addresses the PR review comment: the named renderer interface was
Vulkan-only, and the GL window still constructed the editor's ImGui
renderer itself.

- IeCoreOpenGlInterfaces.Rendering.IOpenGlRenderer : IRenderer with
  CreateImGuiRenderer() — mirrors IVulkanRenderer.
- GL Renderer implements it: SetContext captures IOpenGlContext alongside
  the engine window; OnLoad reads the GL entry points from the captured
  context instead of the SilkNetOpenGlWindow.GetWindowContext static.
- SilkNetOpenGlWindow takes IOpenGlRenderer and hands
  CreateImGuiRenderer() to the editor UI; the direct OpenGLImGuiRenderer
  construction is gone.
- WindowFactory.CreateSilkNetWindow performs the symmetric
  IRenderer -> IOpenGlRenderer downcast.
- CONTEXT.md: architecture line for IOpenGlRenderer.
