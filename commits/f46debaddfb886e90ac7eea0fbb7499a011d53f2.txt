feat(cameras): third-person pull-in raycast wall avoidance (#60)
The third-person rig casts a ray from the pivot along the view axis and
shortens the distance the camera sits at when geometry is between them.

- CurrentDistance (effective) is split from Distance (the player's target),
  so a pull-in can release back to the wheel's distance.
- CameraRigMath.PullInDistance is the pure clamp(hit - radius, min, desired).
- Pull-in is an immediate clamp (never clip); recovery waits
  RecoveryDelayFrames clear frames then eases at RecoverySmoothing, so a
  ray that flickers at a grazing angle cannot make the camera pump.
- Optional IScenePhysicsQueries ctor dependency; null keeps the plain orbit
  camera. Registered concretely in DI for the runtime camera mode (#59).
- FakeScenePhysicsQueries + unit tests for the wall, grazing flicker,
  near/far hit jumps, recovery and the pure distance rule.
